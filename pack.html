Pack

Escape a surreal bunker, one dangerous room at a time, while its twisted host turns your struggle into his show.

Engine
Unreal Engine
Genre
Escape / dark comedy
Experience
Single-player escape game
Core hook
A host who watches every move
The Game Idea

A game you never agreed to play

You wake up alone in a locked facility with no idea how you got there. A voice introduces the rules: escape each room to get closer to freedom—and to the prize The Pack keeps promising. Every room is a new puzzle, trap or test. You have to study your surroundings, take risks and figure out how the room works before it gets the better of you.

What makes the game

A room-by-room challenge

Each locked room has its own puzzle, trap or dangerous test. The challenges grow more demanding as you move deeper into the facility, keeping every new door a question mark.

The Pack is always watching

Through an earpiece, loudspeakers and screens, The Pack gives instructions, reacts to your choices and taunts you when you fail. He treats your fear like entertainment—and makes even a simple room feel personal.

Dark comedy in a strange show

The facility is built from stark, box-like rooms, touched by purple lights and subtle circus details. Its unsettling game-show feel mixes real tension with The Pack's absurd, cruel humour.

Failure is part of the game

Losing a challenge sends you back to the start of that room, not the beginning of the whole game. You can try a different approach while The Pack comments on your failure. That keeps the stakes tense without making experimentation feel like a waste of progress.

There is more behind the rooms

The Pack is not just the voice announcing each challenge: he is the mastermind behind the facility and its spectacle. Small robots prepare and maintain the rooms, making sure the show can continue. But one room is still under construction. It offers a chance to slip into the hidden corridors, break out of the challenge loop and discover what The Pack is really doing.

The host himself is a strange mix of showman and menace. His obsession with games began with family tragedies tied to games of Ludo and Bully, shaping his desire to turn games into dangerous spectacles. Most of the time he stays out of sight; when he does appear, it might be on a screen—or even on a hoverboard.

Escape the show

The Pack promises a cash prize, but the deeper you go, the more the game becomes about taking back control. Will you play by his rules—or use the unfinished room to find a way out?
