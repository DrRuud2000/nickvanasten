About me

Personal Info

Game developer & creative thinker

Hi, I'm Nick, an ICT student at Fontys and a game developer with a passion for dark, atmospheric worlds. I enjoy building immersive experiences and combining creative ideas with technical problem-solving.

A bit more about me

I have always had a vivid imagination and enjoy turning original ideas into ambitious experiences. I have built with LEGO for as long as I can remember, often creating things from scratch without instructions and relying on an idea in my head to bring them to life.

I also designed Fortnite and Minecraft maps for friends and for myself to play. These included hide-and-seek and murder-mystery experiences, where I could build places and ideas for other people to explore.

Video editing and storytelling

I have been making videos and developing ideas for as long as I can remember. During the COVID-19 pandemic, I ran a TikTok account that grew to 4.6K followers. I enjoy shaping an idea into a finished video through editing, pacing and storytelling.
