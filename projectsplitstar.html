A small recreation of the segmented layout (12 segments of 10 HP). It is not the game build.

Segmented Health

My main individual contribution. Inspired by My Friend Pedro, I split health into visible segments instead of one continuous bar, so players can read damage quickly in busy fights. A PlayerHealth component stores current and maximum health (120 HP) and handles damage and healing, while the UI updates immediately when health changes. The Bastion shield is handled separately and absorbs damage before regular health is touched.
